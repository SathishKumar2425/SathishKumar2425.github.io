About

Researcher turned builder

I spent my PhD at Rani Channamma University applying deep learning to biometric data analysis, determining age and gender from handwritten signatures and fingerprints, and extended that work to fetal ultrasound analysis. Along the way I published 11 peer-reviewed papers and released two public datasets on Mendeley Data. I currently teach computer science as an Assistant Professor at Amity University Bengaluru, and I am moving into industry as an AI Engineer / Data Scientist, with a focus on computer vision, applied ML, and GenAI/RAG systems.

Age & Gender from Handwritten Signatures

CNN (VGG16, transfer learning) and feature-fusion models that classify a writer's age group and gender from offline signature images. Trained on a self-collected corpus of 6,010 signatures from 610 individuals.
